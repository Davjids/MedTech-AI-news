## 🫀 Key Clinical AI Breakthroughs

*   **Sub-10ms Intraoperative OCT-IVUS Co-Registration for Robotic TAVI:** A hybrid spatial-temporal Vision Transformer (ViT) achieves real-time, frame-level segmentation of asymmetric calcification patterns and neo-sinus anatomy. Trained on over 12,000 intravascular volumetric frames, the model predicts paravalvular leak (PVL) risks post-balloon expansion with an AUROC of 0.94, directly streaming dynamic spatial guidance vectors to a robotic catheter delivery platform.
*   **Predictive LCOS Trajectory via Cross-Attention Waveform Embeddings:** A self-supervised transformer network integrating high-frequency (250 Hz) radial arterial line waveforms with continuous multi-lead ECG forecasts Low Cardiac Output Syndrome (LCOS) up to 4 hours prior to clinical manifestation. By encoding latent dynamics of $dP/dt_{max}$ degradation and beat-to-beat pulse pressure variations, the architecture improves predictive sensitivity by 38% over traditional threshold-based systems.

## ⚡ Engineering & Computational Mechanics

*   **PINN Surrogates for Real-Time Thoracic Aortic Hemodynamics:** To resolve the computational bottleneck of full-order Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), a Navier-Stokes-constrained Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) reconstructs 3D velocity fields and dynamic pressure gradients directly from sparse, noisy 4D-Flow MRI data. 
*   The network evaluates Time-Averaged Wall Shear Stress (TAWSS) and Oscillatory Shear Index (OSI) across complex thoracic aortic aneurysms in <45 ms per cardiac cycle. By embedding non-linear hyperelastic vessel wall mechanics directly into the loss function, this surrogate models Fluid-Structure Interaction (FSI) during Thoracic Endovascular Aortic Repair (TEVAR), mapping localized endograft displacement forces driven by convective fluid acceleration.

*   **Precision Structural Heart Interventions:** Real-time calcification and stress distribution mapping allow operators to optimize transcatheter valve positioning depth and inflation volume intraoperatively, significantly reducing post-procedural regurgitation and permanent pacemaker implantation rates in non-tricuspid aortic anatomy.
*   **Intraoperative Digital Twin Integration:** Sub-50ms hemodynamic surrogates transition biomechanical digital twins from post-hoc analysis into real-time operating room tools, enabling surgeons to virtually stress-test endograft sizing, conformability, and altered wall shear patterns prior to stent deployment.
*   **Targeted ICU Hemodynamic Resuscitation:** Early LCOS trajectory alerts facilitate preemptive, goal-directed therapy—balancing volume administration against vasopressor titrations—before systemic hypoperfusion triggers post-cardiotomy acute kidney injury (AKI) or prolonged mechanical ventilation.
